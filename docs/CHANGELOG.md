# What's new in Dream Team Dex

## 🐟 Pokémon that act like themselves
- **Water Pokémon live in the pond:** Water types spend most of the day swimming and drifting (and sleep there too),
  and fish like Magikarp, Goldeen and Wailmer never leave it. On rainy days they head out into the garden. Pokémon
  that can't swim walk round the pond instead of bumping into it.
- **Fliers fly:** birds take off and flap when they go somewhere, floaters hover higher, and both perch on top of tall
  furniture (bookshelves, statues, clocks, the fridge, trees) and sleep up there.
- **Every type has favourite spots:** Fire types warm up at the fireplace and stove, Grass types sunbathe in the yard,
  Ice types visit the snowman and keep away from the heat, Fighting types train on the punching bag and dummy, Electric
  types watch TV and play the arcade, Psychic types gaze at crystal balls, and so on.
- **Rooms make sense:** outdoor types go out in the morning and indoor types stay in; when it rains most come inside.
  At bedtime everyone heads for the bedroom (or a sofa). Fed a berry, a Pokémon takes it to the table or counter to eat.
- **Size matters:** big Pokémon like Snorlax don't squeeze onto chairs, sofas or cushions; tiny ones love cushions.

## 🏡 A real house
- **Front yard and back garden:** your Home now starts in the front yard, by the street. Out in a yard you see your house
  from outside (roof, front wall, windows), and it opens up as you walk in through the door.
- **House designs** in the Shop: the free Cozy cottage, a Brick townhouse, a Log cabin and a Seaside villa.
- **A new room, the Kitchen**, with a stove, a retro fridge and a kitchen counter to furnish it. Rooms now run front yard →
  bedroom → living room → kitchen → Grand hall → garden → pond, and your furniture moves along with its room.
- **Nicer doorways** between rooms: carved door posts, a lantern-lit front door, rose-covered garden gates and doormats.
- **Your PokéPoints stay in view** at the top of the Shop while you scroll.
- **Decorating is easier:** drag the floor (or use the arrow keys) to look around, jump to any room, and tap a piece of
  furniture for a toolbar right above it: Move, Flip, Turn, Put away. Drag it to slide it or nudge it with the arrow keys;
  new pieces show up in view, ready to place, and Esc puts a moved piece back.
- **Choose who lives here:** the new Trainer button picks the trainer you walk around your Home as (friends see them too).
- **Beat a region's League, bring any team:** once you've beaten a region's Elite Four and Champion, its Gyms and League
  take any of your teams.
- **Pokémon switch style with you:** flipping between 2D and 3D now changes the sprites of everyone in your Home too.

## 🎨 All-new Home art
- **Every Home item redrawn** as detailed pixel art (2D), and nine more pieces animate: the fountain, fireplace, fish tank,
  TV, arcade, grandfather clock, holiday tree, jack-o'-lantern and weather vane.
- **Pokémon face the way they walk** in the Home (they used to walk backwards half the time). Your trainer too.
- **Badge Cases show the real badges.** Walk up to one and press E (on a phone, the action button) to see each badge up close with its Gym
  Leader. The Gym screen shows them too.
- **Four new Hall of Fame backgrounds** in the Shop: Stadium lights, Sky Pillar, Blossom shrine and Coral reef.

## ✨ Furniture that moves (2D)
- Twelve Home pieces that used to be simple drawings are now detailed, animated pixel art: the Tesla lamp crackles,
  the lava lamp's wax drifts, the lighthouse lamp glows, torches and the ghost lantern flicker, the crystal, cosmic
  and beauty orbs swirl and sparkle, the honey cauldron bubbles, the seashell fountain pours and the volcano hot tub
  steams. (3D keeps its own drawings.)

## 💥 Every move has an animation
- **All 851 moves animate** in 3D and 2D: flames, waves, sparks, quakes, slashes, beams, stat-up glows, screens and
  hazards, plus hand-made effects for signature moves. Contact moves lunge; everything else casts.
- **Wide-winged Pokémon at a fair size:** Charizard, Yveltal and friends were drawn small because their wings filled the
  box. Sprites are now sized by their body, so they stand as tall as everyone else.

## ▶ Battle replays
- **Rewatch your last battles** from Battle → Replays (or "▶ Replay" when a battle ends). A replay re-runs the battle
  from its saved choices, so it plays out exactly as it happened.
- **Share a replay as a link**, and pin one as your **Battle of the Week** on your trainer card for friends to watch.

## 📋 Showdown import and export
- Team options → **Export to Showdown** gives your team in Pokémon Showdown's format; **Import from Showdown** reads a
  Showdown team (forms, nicknames, items, abilities, moves, natures, EVs, IVs, level, shiny) into a new team or the
  current one, and lists anything it couldn't use.

## 🗼 The Battle Tower
- **How long can you last?** Battles in a row with one team, 3 on 3 or 6 on 6 at level 50, healed between battles.
  Trainers get tougher every 7 wins, and every 7th battle is **Tower Tycoon Palmer**.
- Wins pay more as your streak grows. Streaks of **21 and 49** earn Tower-only rewards: two profile icons, a Battle Tower
  background and a steel trophy for your Home. They're never sold.

## 🏠 More Home
- **Games:** **Berry Catch** (catch falling berries with one of your Pokémon and keep some) and **Hide-and-seek** (find
  the Pokémon hiding behind your furniture; hints if you're stuck). Both grow friendship.
- **New furniture:** a striped **Hammock** to nap in, and **Holiday gifts** for the winter holidays.
- **Sleepier sleepers:** sleeping sprites are sized closer to the awake Pokémon, and species without one curl up and
  breathe slowly.

## ⚡ Faster, and works offline
- The first visit downloads far less (the 2D art now loads just after the first screen), repeat visits open about
  twice as fast, and the app starts with no connection. Updates only re-download sprite packs that changed.

## 📅 Daily missions and a login streak
- **Three missions a day** that fit how you play (win with a Water type, feed 3 berries, play the Daily 10…), each worth
  PokéPoints, with a bonus for all three.
- **Visit every day** for a streak: a little each day, more every 7th day and on day 30. Miss a day and you keep half.

## ⚔️ Battle your friends, live
- **Challenge a friend who's online** from your friends list or their profile: pick 1 vs 1, 3 vs 3 or 6 vs 6, and
  whether to bring the Bag. They get a challenge card with Accept / Not now.
- **Real time, both of you in control:** you each pick your own moves, switches and items, and every turn plays out
  on both screens at once. Everyone is level 50, and your Pokémon show their backs in 3D.
- **Fair and tidy:** a 60-second turn timer (it picks for you if time runs out), and if someone's connection drops
  for 30 seconds, the other player wins. Rematch with one tap.
- **Rewards:** a win is worth 60 PokéPoints (up to 3 paid wins against each friend a day), plus two new
  achievements: Friendly rivals and Best of friends.

## 🏠 A Pokémon Home that feels alive
- **Real sleeping Pokémon:** napping Pokémon curl up with proper sleeping sprites (969 species, from PMD Sprite
  Collab) instead of a squashed sprite.
- **Speech bubbles:** messages show the Pokémon's icon, name and friendship hearts, and the words type in. Hover or
  tap a Pokémon to see its name; prompts are neat "E · Play with Pikachu" key-caps.
- **Light and shadow:** soft shadows sized to each Pokémon (pixel shadows in 2D), furniture that sits on the floor,
  sunlight through the windows, and sunsets and nights that fade in with the clock. Lamps and fires pool warm light.
- **Livelier Pokémon:** they walk smoothly around each other, face what they're doing (in 3D they turn their backs
  to watch TV or warm up by the fire), chase each other when playing, and friends trail behind you and stop when
  you stop. Petting bursts with hearts; feeding munches; picked berries fly into your pouch; gifts wobble and pop
  open with confetti. Rain splashes and snow dusts the ground outside.
- **Bedtime that works:** at night, everyone who isn't nocturnal heads for the nearest bed.
- **Tidier toolbar** with icons, a PokéPoints pill, and resident cards with friendship hearts.

## ✨ Cleaner everywhere
- **No more messy rows:** type badges, tabs and buttons line up evenly instead of one-then-four, names wrap instead of
  getting cut off, and nothing pokes out of its box. An automatic checker now looks at every screen in 2D and 3D,
  on desktop, tablet and phone, before each update.
- **One consistent look:** the same text sizes, spacing, button heights and shadows on every screen; numbers line up;
  sprites show a soft placeholder while they load; messages slide in at the top so they never cover your buttons.
- **Easier to use:** clear focus outlines for keyboard players, better contrast on small text, and screen-reader
  labels checked automatically.
- **Small fixes:** the trainer card's "VIII" no longer overlaps, battle portraits fit on phones, the stat card no
  longer covers your team, and the sign-in email wording matches the email you get.

**Site size:** 419.8 MB in 608 files (GitHub Pages allows 1 GB; the largest file is well under 100 MB).

# Earlier: the first big update
Everything from the first big update, in plain words.

## ⚔️ Battles that play like the real games
- **Real damage:** the real Pokémon damage formula. It's checked against the community damage calculator on tens of
  thousands of random battles and matches exactly.
- **Moves with PP:** pick your moves each turn, see PP and effectiveness, and run out like in the games.
- **Abilities, items and field effects:** Intimidate, Levitate, Leftovers, Life Orb, berries, weather, terrain,
  screens, status and stat changes all work.
- **The Bag:** use Potions, Revives, Full Heals and X items mid-battle. Elite Four runs have a limited Bag.
- **Smarter opponents:** Gym Leaders, the Elite Four and Champions get tougher as you go.

## 🧰 A proper team builder
- Tap a Pokémon for its sheet. The **Battle** tab has ability, held item, level, nature, EVs/IVs and moves.
  **Personalize** is for looks; **Info** is the Pokédex.
- A **move picker** you can sort and filter (STAB only, by power…), plus a searchable **item picker** with real bag
  icons.
- Mega Evolutions can't hold items, and there are no Mega Stones.

## 🏆 Rewards that make sense
- **Gym Leaders:** every one gives a reward that fits them (Brock a fossil display, Misty a shell fountain, special
  Poké Balls…), plus an icon and a badge case for each region.
- **Elite Four:** each member gives a statue and an elite icon the first time you beat them.
- **Champions:** a card design, a Champion's Hall background, a trophy, a title and an icon.
- **Already beat some?** If you beat leaders before this update, their rewards are waiting for you.
- **Exclusive:** these rewards can't be bought in the Shop.

## ✨ 3D sprites at their real size
- 3D sprites are the animated model renders, shown exactly as made: no blurry upscaling.
- In battle, **your Pokémon now shows its back**, animated.

## ☁️ Play on any device
- **Sign in with Google or an email code**, pick a username and get a friend code.
- **Your progress follows you** to every browser. It saves on your device first, so it works offline, and syncs in
  the background.
- **No progress lost by accident:** if two devices both changed, you choose which progress to keep, and your account
  remembers your last 20 saves.
- **Bring your old saves:** on your first sign-in, choose which save file to carry into your account.
- **No account needed:** guest play and save files without an account still work.
- **Your data, your call:** download all of it, or delete your account any time.

## 👋 Friends
- **Add friends** by friend code, username or a share link.
- **See their profile:** their trainer card, the team they chose to show off (its Hall of Fame card plus a lineup with
  sprites, nicknames, Poké Balls, items and moves), and their badges.
- **Copy their team,** or **battle it** with the computer playing their side.
- **Visit their Pokémon Home,** say hi to their Pokémon and leave a **Like** once a day: you both get 20 PokéPoints.
- **Kid-safe:** no chat or messages, usernames are filtered, and blocking hides you completely.

## 🏠 A Home that feels alive
- **They use your furniture:** Pokémon nap in beds, sit on sofas, watch TV, warm up by the fire, bounce on the
  trampoline and splash in fountains, each by type (Fire loves the fireplace, Water the fountain…).
- **They play together:** friends meet up, especially Pokémon from the same evolution family.
- **Day and night:** at night most go to sleep, Ghost and Dark types roam, and lamps, fires and TVs glow.
- **Berries:** Berry trees grow berries to pick. Feed them to your Pokémon; each has a secret favourite flavour.
- **Friendship milestones:**
  - 25: a special emote.
  - 50: it follows you around.
  - 60+: it sometimes leaves you a gift.
  - 100: a friendship ribbon on its card.
- **Decorating:** drag furniture to move it, Undo mistakes, turn rugs.
- **Photo mode:** save a picture or a GIF of your Home.
- **Weather and seasons:** daily weather outside the windows. **Halloween is on now** (Oct 15 – Nov 1): pumpkins and
  cobwebs in the Shop, spooky purple nights and Ghost-type visitors.
- **Sound:** new calm Home music and sound effects.
- **Phones:** a joystick and a Play button.

## 🎖️ New achievements
First friend, Social circle, House guest, Talk of the town, Berry good, Ribbon collector and Home photographer.
